## Mixamo — unrecoverable via script

`models/fbx/mixamo/` (~3000 stubs — animations + riggs). These require [mixamo.com](https://www.mixamo.com/) login and per-animation downloads. No API. To restore:

1. Log in with your Adobe account.
2. Re-download the characters/animations you had.
3. Names in the stub paths hint at what was there (e.g. `inward-slash-left.glb`, `sword and shield strafe.glb`).

## One-click recovery scripts

Shell scripts I wrote live in `/tmp/`:
- `/tmp/download_bundle.sh <bundle-path> <target-dir>` — pulls every model from a poly.pizza bundle
- `/tmp/download_orphans.sh <dir>` — re-downloads stubs that contain an embedded `-{id}` in the filename
- `/tmp/run_all_bundles.sh` + `/tmp/bundle_map.txt` — batch runner for many bundles at once

To grab more bundles: just add lines to `bundle_map.txt` in the form `/bundle/<Name-ID>|<target-folder-name>` and re-run.
